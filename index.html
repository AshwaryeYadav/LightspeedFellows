Program Overview

The program will run concurrently with the Berkeley 2025-2026 academic calendar.

The Lightspeed Fellows Program is designed to provide exceptional technical students with direct mentorship from our partners and access to our extensive network of founders and industry leaders.

Direct Mentorship

Work directly with Lightspeed partners who will provide hands-on guidance throughout your fellowship experience.

Network Access

Connect with fellow students, alumni, and professionals across the Lightspeed & wider technology ecosystem.

Career Opportunities

Exclusive access to internships, full-time roles, and startup opportunities within our network.

Learning Resources

Monthly workshops, events, and educational content designed to accelerate your professional development.

Hands-On Partner Involvement

Our partners will be actively involved with the cohort, providing direct mentorship and guidance throughout the program.

Who Should Apply

We're looking for exceptional students passionate about technology and innovation

Eligibility Criteria

  • Currently enrolled undergraduate or graduate student
  • Strong academic performance and demonstrated technical skills
  • Passion for technology, innovation, and entrepreneurship
  • Leadership experience or potential for leadership
  • Available to participate in program activities and events

What We Look For

We welcome all applicants who have demonstrated strong technical foundations and leadership potential. We also look for an interest in startups, though prior experience is not required.
